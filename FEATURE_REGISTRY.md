# AI Product Compliance Evidence Vault Feature Registry

- `evidence-vault` - Evidence Vault (Evidence): Artifacts, owners, product areas, control links, retention, and audit status.
- `model-card-registry` - Model Card Registry (AI Governance): Model cards, intended use, limitations, evals, risks, and approvals.
- `release-approval-history` - Release Approval History (Release): Release gates, approvers, evidence, exceptions, and go/no-go decisions.
- `security-test-evidence` - Security Test Evidence (Security): Pen tests, scans, findings, remediation, retest status, and proof.
- `privacy-review-evidence` - Privacy Review Evidence (Privacy): DPIAs, data maps, lawful basis, retention, and privacy sign-off.
- `ai-evaluation-records` - AI Evaluation Records (AI Governance): Eval suites, results, thresholds, regressions, and deployment decisions.
- `control-crosswalk` - Control Crosswalk (Controls): Framework requirements, controls, evidence links, gaps, and owner actions.
- `customer-audit-packets` - Customer Audit Packets (Customer Trust): Questionnaires, evidence bundles, redactions, approvals, and delivery log.
- `exception-register` - Exception Register (Risk): Accepted gaps, risk owner, expiry, compensating controls, and review cadence.
- `compliance-dashboard` - Compliance Dashboard (Reporting): Evidence coverage, open gaps, upcoming audits, risk trends, and readiness score.
- `documents` - Documents
- `notifications` - Notifications
- `integrations` - Integrations
- `profiles` - Profiles
- `ai-assistant` - AI Assistant
- `ai-tools` - AI Tools
